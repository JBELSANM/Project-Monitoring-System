A. CLEARING AND GRUBBING WORKS
1. Limits of work are already established and is within the approved property line
—
2. Trees and other plants for retention are tagged or marked with paint
—
3. Surface objects, stumps, roots and other protruding obstructions not designated to remain are cleared and/or grubbed or mowed as required.
—
4. Top soil on areas covered by cogon/talabih, wild grass and other vegetation is cut to a maximum depth of 150 mm below the original ground surface or as designated by the Engineer
—
5. Low hanging branches and unsound or unsightly branches on trees or shrubs designated to remain are trimmed as directed.
—
6. Permit for cutting or balling out of trees is already issued by the responsible authority
—
B. LAY-OUT AND STAKING WORKS
7. Identified location of existing utilities particularly those underground utilities are marked or tagged
—
8. The baseline/reference point to which location of the building corners are to be referred is established.
—
9. Reference baseline is fixed, stable or immovable structure
—
10. Established stakes/pegs and batter boards are stable
—
11. Stakes and batter boards are of good quality lumber, steel, pipes or as approved by the Engineer
—
12. String lines to distinguish the dimensions of the building layout are established.
—
13. Horizontal and vertical controls are established, true to line, plumb and square
—
14. Vertical and Horizontal Control within tolerances
—
15. Appropriate surveying instrument is used
—
16. Established on site layout conforms to the plan, required setback from the property line and existing right of way
—
C. CONSTRUCTION SAFETY AND HEALTH REQUIREMENTS
17. Materials and debris which cannot be burned and perishable materials recovered out of site clearing and grubbing are disposed off by methods and locations approved by the Engineer.
—
18. Access for movement and mobilization of equipment particularly for the subsequent excavation works is provided
—
19. Materials for soil protection in case of deep excavations are ready on-site (if necessary)
—
